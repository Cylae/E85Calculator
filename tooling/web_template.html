Equation and limitations

ef = (R × er + X × ep + Y × es) / (R + X + Y)

Upper bounds maximise X under the entered ceiling. X is rounded down to 0.01 L; Y is the modelled remainder. Additive volumes, homogeneous mixing and the entered compositions are assumptions. The displayed content does not predict an engine warning light.
